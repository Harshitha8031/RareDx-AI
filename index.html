Frequently Asked Questions

How does RareDx AI shorten the Diagnostic Odyssey?

Rare diseases average 4-7 years of misdiagnosis because no single doctor has expertise across 7,000 disorders. RareDx AI simulates an instant Multi-Disciplinary Team (MDT) of 6 specialists working together in parallel with Orphanet and HPO knowledge.

How is patient data kept private?

The system runs entirely on localhost using Ollama for LLaMA 3, PostgreSQL for persistence, and ChromaDB for vector retrieval. Zero patient data leaves the workstation.

What happens if the local LLM server is unavailable?

RareDx AI features a built-in deterministic heuristic fallback scoring engine that calculates the Top-5 differential directly from Orphanet profiles without requiring the LLM.

5-Stage System Architecture & Methodology

RareDx AI imitates the clinical Multi-Disciplinary Team (MDT) consultation through a structured five-stage automated pipeline:

1

NLP & Extraction

Tokenization, 3-token negation detection, synonym mapping, temporal onset extraction, and HPO ontology matching (19,389 terms).

2

Knowledge Retrieval

Matching feature vectors to 30 curated Orphanet disease profiles and querying NCBI PubMed E-utilities with 24-hour caching.

3

Multi-Agent MDT

6 specialist agents (GP 1.0x, Neuro 1.2x, Genetics 1.5x, Radio 1.1x, Immuno 1.2x, Meta 1.3x) analyze the case in parallel via LLaMA 3.

4

Consensus Discussion

Two-round discussion protocol evaluates agreement. If consensus < 60%, cross-peer revision and Meta-Agent arbitration are triggered.

5

Decision Fusion

Weighted scoring combining keyword overlap, must-have matches, onset bonus (+0.15), family bonus (+0.10), and exclusion penalties (-2.0).

01 / MOTIVATION & PROBLEM

The Rare Disease Diagnostic Odyssey

A rare disease affects <1 in 2,000 individuals, but over 7,000 rare diseases exist globally, impacting 300 million patients (50% children).

  • Severe Diagnostic Delay: Average patient waits 4 to 7 years for a definitive diagnosis.
  • Multiple Misdiagnoses: Consults 5 to 8 specialists and undergoes 2 to 3 misdiagnoses before confirmation.
  • Specialist Scarcity: No single clinician can master 7,000 disorders with overlapping phenotypes.
  • Cloud Privacy Barriers: Hospital data cannot be transmitted to proprietary cloud LLMs due to HIPAA and data protection laws.

03 / METHODOLOGY & ARCHITECTURE

5-Stage Multi-Agent System Pipeline

1

Clinical NLP & HPO

Extracts phenotypes with 3-token negation filtering (denies, no, negative for) and synonym normalization.

2

Vector Retrieval

Embeds patient features and queries Orphanet profiles + NCBI PubMed with 24-hr local caching.

3

Parallel MDT Debates

6 domain agents independently analyze patient context according to specialized weights (1.0x to 1.5x).

4

Consensus Protocol

Evaluates round 1 consensus. If <60%, initiates cross-specialist peer review and Meta-Agent arbitration.

5

Decision Fusion

Applies mathematical scoring, onset matching, inheritance bonuses, exclusion penalties, and rank discounting.

Specialist Agent Weights & Decision Fusion Scoring

Mathematical Scoring Formula (Algorithm 1)

Score(d) = 0.60 * KW(d) + 0.40 * MustHave(d)
         + 0.15 * OnsetMatch(d)
         + 0.10 * FamilyHistory(d)
         - 2.00 * Exclusions(d)

Rank Discount: [1.0, 0.75, 0.55, 0.40, 0.28]

Guarantees transparent explainability with recommended confirmatory diagnostic laboratory panels.
